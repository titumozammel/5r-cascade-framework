The 5R Decision Model

REMOVE
Eliminate unnecessary demand entirely — If it doesn't need to exist, remove it.
REDUCE
Decrease frequency or volume — Less of the same work.
REPLACE
Shift execution model — Self-service, automation, or different owner.
RE-ENGINEER
Redesign operating logic — Make demand structurally disappear.
RETAIN
Lock + Monitor — Sustain operational silence.

⚡ Side Rules: Never reduce what you can remove • Never replace what you can reduce • Always start at the top • Re-engineering is last resort

⚡ Core Principle

Energy — not time — is the real organizational constraint.

Human energy is the only constraint that does not scale with budget.

Protective Friction

Some friction exists to prevent catastrophic failure. Do not eliminate it. Contain it.

Contain = ACCEPT → ISOLATE → BUFFER

  • ACCEPT — Acknowledge it must exist (e.g., compliance review)
  • ISOLATE — Separate from main flow (dedicated queue)
  • BUFFER — Absorb variability (batch processing)

The Rule: Eliminate waste friction. Preserve protective friction.

Getting Started

  3. Apply the 5R hierarchy from top to bottom (REMOVE first!)
  4. Measure operational silence (demand reduction, handoff reduction)
  5. Repeat every 4 weeks with a constraint audit
